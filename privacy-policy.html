Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CosmoZen Decor (“CosmoZen”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit this website, request a quote, place an order or contact us. We handle personal data in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as they apply to us.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and delivery address when you enquire, order or message us.
  • Order details: materials, sizes, quantities, design preferences, and any sketches, photos or files you send us for a custom piece.
  • Payment details: online payments are processed by our payment partner (Razorpay), and direct payments are made by UPI. We do not see or store your card, net-banking or UPI PIN details. We keep a record of the payment reference and amount.
  • Messages: what you send us on WhatsApp or email, so we can answer you and keep a record of what was agreed.
  • Basic technical data: your browser and device type and the pages you visit, as seen by the services that host this website and deliver its fonts and payment widget.

2. How we use your information

  • To prepare quotes, confirm orders, make and deliver your pieces.
  • To take payments, issue refunds and keep accounting and tax records.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to meet legal obligations.
  • To send updates about new designs or offers, only if you have asked for them. You can opt out at any time.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what is needed with: our payment partner; courier and logistics partners (your name, phone number and delivery address); messaging providers such as WhatsApp when you chat with us; hosting and content providers that run this website (for example GitHub Pages and Google Fonts); our professional advisers such as accountants; and government or legal authorities where the law requires it.

4. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your shopping cart is saved in your browser’s local storage on your own device so it is still there when you return. Clear your browser data to remove it. Third-party services we load, such as Google Fonts and Razorpay checkout, may set their own cookies or collect technical data under their own policies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to complete your order, handle any support or complaint, and meet legal requirements such as tax and accounting records. If you sent us designs or photos for a custom piece, you can ask us to delete them once your order is complete.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw any consent you have given. Email or message us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
