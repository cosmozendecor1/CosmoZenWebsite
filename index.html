ABOUT US

We cut things for a living. Precisely.

CosmoZen Decor is a laser-cut studio that turns flat sheets of MDF and acrylic into wall art, partitions and custom pieces. Our laser is accurate to a fraction of a millimetre. Our jokes, slightly less so.

Buying decor online can feel like a guessing game: a “call for price” that never gets a call back, fixed sizes that never fit your wall, and a delivery date that is more of a suggestion. So we built a shop that works the other way round.

Pick a material, type in your size, and see the price before you talk to anyone. Want something no catalogue has? Send us a sketch, a photo or a half-formed idea and we will cut it. Interior designers, architects and hospitality teams are very welcome too.
