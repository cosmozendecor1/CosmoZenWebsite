Shipping Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how and when your CosmoZen order reaches you.

1. Where we ship

We ship across India. We do not offer international shipping at the moment. If you need it, message us and we will see what is possible.

2. Production time

Pieces are made to order. Standard pieces usually take 7 to 10 working days from design confirmation and receipt of your advance. Custom, intricate and large-format work is quoted individually, and busy festival periods can take longer. We confirm your timeline in writing.

3. Delivery time

After dispatch, delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days to metro cities and 5 to 8 business days to other locations in India. These are estimates and can change with courier conditions.

4. Shipping charges

Prices shown on our website and in our calculator do not include transport. Shipping cost depends on the size, weight, packaging and destination of your order. We confirm it separately in your quote before you pay.

5. Packaging

We pack flat panels with protective packaging. Large or delicate pieces may need reinforced packs or crates, which we will quote for you.

6. Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, we share the courier and tracking details with you on WhatsApp or email.

7. Delivery address

Please give us a complete address with PIN code and a working phone number. If delivery fails because the address is wrong or nobody is available, re-delivery charges may apply.

8. Damaged in transit

Please check your parcel on delivery. If it looks damaged, contact us within 48 hours with photos of the piece and packaging, and an unboxing video if you have one. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for what happens next.

9. Delays

Courier disruption, weather, festivals and other events outside our control can delay delivery. We will keep you informed if that happens.

10. Questions

Message us on WhatsApp or email and we will help.
