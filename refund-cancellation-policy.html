Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Almost every CosmoZen piece is made to order for you, cut to your size and in your chosen material, so our policy is different from ready-stock shops. We want to be fair to you and to the work.

1. Cancelling an order

  • Before production starts: you can cancel and we will refund what you have paid. Tell us on WhatsApp or email as soon as you can, and we will tell you when production begins on your order.
  • After production has started: your piece is already being cut to your specification, so the booking advance is not refundable.
  • If we cannot make or deliver your order for any reason on our side, we refund everything you have paid.

2. Returns and exchanges

Made-to-order and custom items cannot be returned or exchanged because you changed your mind, chose a different size or design, or gave us incorrect measurements.

3. When we will repair, replace or refund

If your order arrives with any of these problems, we will make it right:

  • It does not match your confirmed order (wrong size, material, thickness or design).
  • It has a manufacturing defect.
  • It was damaged in transit.

4. How to make a claim

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp or email with your order details, clear photos of the piece and its packaging, and an unboxing video if you have one. Please keep the packaging until your claim is settled. We may ask you for more information, or to send the piece back at our cost.

5. What we will do

After reviewing your claim, we will repair the piece, re-cut and replace it, or refund you, whichever is practical. Where we replace a piece, it goes back into production straight away.

6. Refund timelines

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after approval. Your bank may take a little longer to show it. Payments made directly by UPI are refunded to the same UPI ID.

7. What is not covered

  • Minor variations in wood grain, colour or texture, which are normal for MDF and acrylic.
  • Damage from improper installation, moisture, heat, misuse or normal wear.
  • Claims reported after 48 hours of delivery.
  • Sizes, materials or designs that you confirmed but later wish to change.

8. Contact us

Message us on WhatsApp or email us with your order details and we will get back to you quickly.
